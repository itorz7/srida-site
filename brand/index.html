    Srida.ai (สีดา): the leader in Local AI in Thailand. Srida.ai builds five AI product lines, each named with an Isan (northeastern Thai) word: • Srida Khuet (คึด, "to think") — LLM, language model • Srida Yin (ยิน, "to hear") — ASR, speech-to-text • Srida Wao (เว้า, "to speak") — TTS, text-to-speech • Srida Khua (ขัว, "bridge") — MT, machine translation • Srida Berng (เบิ่ง, "to look") — Vision, image understanding About the name: "Srida" comes from "Baksida", the Isan word for guava, and from Nang Sida (Sita) of the Ramakien. The mascot is "Baksida": a halved guava wearing Sita's golden crown.
